Book Library

Discover your next favorite book

Atlas of Lost Suns

Priya Calder

Navigator Iri Sen inherits a forbidden star map that marks the resting places of suns erased from official memory. Pursued across a fractured galaxy, she and her crew race to discover why entire systems were hidden—and what will awaken if the lost suns burn again.

A City Made of Rain

Sophie Mercer

Years after leaving the waterfront city where she lost her first love, Mara Doyle returns for one rain-soaked week that changes everything. As old streets and unfinished conversations pull her back in, she must decide whether memory is a shelter—or a storm she’s never escaped.

Beneath Indigo Tides

Naomi Hart

Marine cartographer Lysa Marr follows moonlit currents to a drowned city said to sing through the water at night. There she discovers a kingdom suspended between ruin and waking, and a choice that could return it to the surface—or bury it forever beneath the tides.

Orchard of Iron Birds

Lena Quill

In a hillside orchard where metal birds nest among blossoming branches, widow Mara Ess keeps hearing messages no one else can hear. When the birds begin repeating the voices of the dead, she follows them toward a forgotten machine hidden under the roots of the valley.

Paper Kingdom, Burning

Felix Rowan

After the royal archives are set ablaze, a minor scribe becomes the only keeper of a prophecy hidden in ash and margin notes. To stop a civil war, he must cross a kingdom where every law is written, rewritten, and weaponized by those who control the ink.

Salt Between the Stones

Jonah Vale

On a storm-battered coast, a stonemason’s daughter returns home after years away to settle her brother’s estate and finds an old feud still poisoning the village. As the sea starts giving up relics from a long-vanished shipwreck, the town must reckon with the lies mortared into its own foundations.

The Clockmaker's Debt

Celia Wren

In a city ruled by contracts and mechanisms, master clockmaker Orin Thatch discovers that his greatest invention was funded by a bargain he never agreed to. With time itself beginning to fracture, he must outwit creditors who collect in years rather than coin.

The Glass Orchard

Mira Solis

In a valley where fruit grows in shimmering glass instead of flesh, botanist Elara Venn is summoned to a fading orchard whose harvest once powered an entire kingdom. As the last luminous branches begin to crack, she uncovers a buried pact between the orchard and a city built on borrowed miracles.

The Last Map of Emberfall

Adrian Skye

A runaway courier steals the final surviving map to Emberfall, a legendary city erased after a war of fire. Hunted by armies and guilds, he journeys through ash-choked passes toward a realm that may still exist—if the map is guiding him to a destination and not a trap.

Winter at Hollowmere

Elias North

When archivist Simon Vale arrives at Hollowmere Manor to catalog a dead woman’s papers, he finds a house sealed by snow, rumor, and grief. Each night reveals a new contradiction in the family’s history, and before the thaw comes he must decide whether the manor is hiding a murderer, a ghost, or both.
